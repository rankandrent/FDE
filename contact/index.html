    Frequently asked questions

    What happens after I submit the form?

    An FDE lead replies within one business day to arrange a 30-minute discovery call. On the call we ask about the outcome you want, the systems involved, and your timeline, then suggest a starting point.

    Do you sign an NDA before the first call?

    Yes, if you prefer. We can sign your NDA or provide ours before any confidential details are shared.

    Is the discovery call free?

    Yes. The discovery call has no cost or commitment. Paid work starts only after scope and price are agreed in writing.

    Can we meet in person?

    Yes. tkxel has offices in the USA, Saudi Arabia, Portugal, and Pakistan, and leads can visit your site for scoping workshops when that helps.
