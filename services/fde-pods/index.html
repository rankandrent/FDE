    Why do embedded teams
     so often stall?

    Extra hands without an owner

    Augmented staff wait for direction, so the outcome still depends on managers who are already at capacity.

    Pilots built outside your stack

    Work done in a separate environment needs rebuilding before it can pass security review.

    Specialist skills you need briefly

    Agent evaluation or data modelling expertise is needed for months, not as a permanent hire.

    No plan for the exit

    When the outside team leaves, nobody inside knows how the system works or how to change it.

    How a pod engagement
     runs from scope to handover

    01

    1. We agree on one measurable outcome, the systems involved, and who on your side will own the result.

      Deliverables: Outcome statement, success metrics, pod shape, access plan

    2. Engineers join your repositories, tickets, and ceremonies under your access controls, paired with your future owners.

      Deliverables: Team charter, working agreement, environment access

    3. The pod releases working increments to staging and then production, reviewed against the agreed metrics every sprint.

      Deliverables: Production releases, sprint reports, architecture records

    4. Your engineers take the lead on changes while the pod reviews, then the pod steps out or moves to the next outcome.

      Deliverables: Runbooks, ownership checklist, enablement sessions

    Frequently asked questions

    What is an FDE pod?

    An FDE pod is a small forward deployed engineering team that works inside your environment to deliver one agreed outcome. It typically includes a lead engineer, two or three specialists, and a shared delivery lead. Unlike staff augmentation, the pod owns the result and plans its own exit from the start.

    How long does a pod usually stay?

    Most pods stay between two and four months for a first outcome. Larger programmes keep a pod longer and move it from one outcome to the next, while ownership of finished systems transfers to your team in stages.

    Can a pod work in our time zone?

    Yes. tkxel has offices in the USA, Saudi Arabia, Portugal, and Pakistan, and pods align their core hours with your team. Pods can work remotely, on-site, or in a hybrid arrangement.

    Who owns the code and IP?

    You do. The pod works in your repositories and cloud accounts from the first commit, under your contracts and access controls. There is nothing to transfer at the end except knowledge, which the handover phase covers.

    When is a pod the wrong choice?

    If your team already has clear technical leadership and only needs more capacity on a known backlog, staff augmentation is usually cheaper. If you have not yet decided what to build, start with the AI strategy and readiness assessment instead. A pod works best when the outcome is clear and internal capacity is not.

    How is pricing structured?

    Pods are priced per engagement phase, based on pod size and duration agreed during scoping. Discovery is fixed-price. We share the full estimate before the pod starts, and changes in scope are agreed in writing rather than billed silently.
