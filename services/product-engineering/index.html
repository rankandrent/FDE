    Frequently asked questions

    How fast can a prototype be ready?

    A focused prototype sprint takes two to four weeks. The prototype is real software that users can test, built with production in mind, though it is not production-ready until security, scale, and evaluation work is done.

    Do you modernise everything at once?

    No. We replace components in order of risk and customer impact while the product keeps running. Full rewrites carry high risk and long delays, so we use them only when a component cannot be improved incrementally.

    Will AI coding tools replace our engineers?

    No. AI assistance speeds up parts of the work, such as boilerplate, tests, and documentation, but design decisions, reviews, and accountability stay with engineers. The SDLC redesign focuses on making that split explicit and safe.

    How do you measure delivery improvement?

    We baseline metrics such as lead time, deployment frequency, change failure rate, and review time before changes, then track them monthly. Results vary by team, and we report them honestly, including where improvement is small.

    Can you work alongside our product team?

    Yes. Pods join your existing product and engineering rituals, and your product managers keep ownership of the roadmap. We bring additional capacity and AI experience rather than a parallel process.

    Which stacks do you work with?

    Common stacks include JavaScript and TypeScript, Python, Java, .NET, Go, and PHP, on AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud. For older stacks we assess the safest modernization path before proposing changes.
