    Is your data ready
     for the AI you plan to ship?

    Your sources disagree on basic facts

    Customer, product and revenue figures differ between CRM, ERP and spreadsheets. A model grounded on that mix repeats the conflict with confidence.

    Pipelines break and nobody notices

    A schema change upstream silently empties a table. Dashboards go stale and the AI feature answers from data that stopped updating last week.

    You cannot trace where a number came from

    When an answer looks wrong, nobody can follow it back to the source system and transformation. Audits and model reviews stall on that question.

    Your data team is stuck on tickets

    Report requests and broken jobs fill the week. The foundations an AI use case needs keep slipping to next quarter.

    What the data pod builds
     for your AI use case

    Data engineering FDE pod

    Source assessment and data contracts

    We map the systems your use case depends on, profile their data and agree contracts with source owners on schema, freshness and ownership. Breaking changes then fail loudly at the boundary instead of corrupting tables downstream.

    Data engineering FDE pod

    Ingestion and ELT pipelines

    We build batch and streaming ingestion from databases, SaaS APIs, files and event streams using tools such as Fivetran, Airbyte, Kafka or native cloud services. Jobs are orchestrated, retried on failure and versioned in your repository.

    Data engineering FDE pod

    Warehouse and lakehouse modeling

    We model your data in Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, Redshift or Postgres with dbt or an equivalent, from staging layers to clean business entities. Each model has an owner, documentation and tests that run with every build.

    Data engineering FDE pod

    Data quality tests and observability

    We add tests for nulls, duplicates, ranges and referential integrity, plus freshness and volume monitors. Alerts route to the owning team, and failed checks stop bad data before it reaches reports or model inputs.

    Data engineering FDE pod

    Lineage and metadata catalog

    We capture column-level lineage from source to dashboard and AI feature, with owners, freshness and quality status attached. When an answer looks wrong, your team can trace it back to the exact job and source record.

    Data engineering FDE pod

    AI-ready feature and retrieval layers

    We prepare the datasets your AI uses: feature tables for models, and cleaned, chunked, permission-tagged documents for retrieval. Refresh schedules keep embeddings in step with source changes, so answers do not drift from current records.

    Data engineering FDE pod

    Governance, access and cost controls

    We set role-based access, masking for sensitive fields and retention rules that match your compliance needs. Warehouse usage monitoring and query tuning keep compute spend visible as data volume and AI workloads grow.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are data engineering services for AI?

    Data engineering services for AI build the pipelines, storage, quality controls and lineage that give models and AI features accurate, current and permitted data. That includes ingesting from source systems, modeling data in a warehouse or lakehouse, testing it on every run and preparing feature tables or document collections for retrieval. A tkxel data pod does this for one AI use case at a time, inside your own cloud and tools.

    What does AI-ready data mean?

    AI-ready data is data that is clean, consistent, documented with lineage, refreshed on a known schedule and access-controlled for the way a model or AI feature will use it. A warehouse built for monthly reports is often not enough, because AI features need fresher data, permission tags on documents and records that agree across systems. We define readiness against your specific use case rather than your whole data estate.

    Which data tools and platforms do you work with?

    We work with the stack you already run, including Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, Redshift, Azure Synapse and Postgres for storage, dbt for transformation, Airflow, Dagster or cloud-native schedulers for orchestration, and Fivetran, Airbyte or Kafka for ingestion. For data quality we use dbt tests, Great Expectations or Soda, and we connect to observability tools you already license. We recommend a new tool only when an existing one cannot meet the use case.

    How do you handle data quality and lineage?

    We handle data quality with tests and data contracts that run inside the pipelines, and lineage by capturing column-level dependencies from source to output. Tests check nulls, duplicates, ranges, relationships and freshness, and a failed check stops the load rather than passing bad records downstream. Lineage carries owners and quality status, so when a dashboard or AI answer looks wrong your team can find the responsible job quickly.

    Do we need a new data warehouse before starting an AI project?

    No, most AI projects can start on your existing warehouse, database or lake if the data for the chosen use case is fixed first. We assess whether your current platform can handle the freshness, volume and access rules the use case needs. A migration makes sense only when the current platform blocks those requirements, and we would scope it as a separate outcome rather than a precondition.

    What can a data engineering pod not fix?

    A data engineering pod cannot fix data that is never captured, or business definitions your teams have not agreed on. If sales and finance define revenue differently, we can surface the conflict and model both versions, but someone in your organization has to decide which one the AI should use. Source owners also need to commit to data contracts; without that, pipelines stay fragile no matter how well they are built.

    How do you protect sensitive data during the engagement?

    We protect sensitive data by working inside your environment under your access policies, with least-privilege roles, masking and audit logging. tkxel holds ISO 27001 certification for information security management, and our engineers can work with synthetic or masked data in development when production access is restricted. Data does not leave your cloud accounts unless you approve it.
