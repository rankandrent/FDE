    How the assessment
     runs in four weeks

    01

    1. We interview leaders and the people who run key workflows, and review systems and data sources.

      Deliverables: Stakeholder map, interview notes, system inventory

    2. Priority workflows are mapped step by step, with current time, cost, and error rates recorded as the baseline.

      Deliverables: Workflow maps, baseline metrics

    3. Use cases are scored on value, feasibility, data readiness, and risk, and the top candidates get business cases.

      Deliverables: Scored portfolio, business cases, data readiness findings

    4. We sequence the work, define team needs, and present the roadmap to leadership for decision.

      Deliverables: Roadmap, readiness scorecard, executive briefing

    Frequently asked questions

    How long does the assessment take?

    Most assessments take two to four weeks depending on how many departments and workflows are in scope. A single-department assessment can finish in two weeks; a company-wide review usually takes the full four.

    Who from our side needs to be involved?

    An executive sponsor, a few hours from each department lead, and time with the people who run the workflows day to day. We also need read access to the systems and data sources the priority use cases depend on.

    Will you recommend building everything with tkxel?

    No. The roadmap names the best route for each use case, which is sometimes an existing product licence or work your own team can do. Where an embedded pod is the right choice, we say so and explain why.

    What if our data is not ready?

    That is a common and useful finding. The data readiness audit sizes each gap, and the roadmap orders work so that data foundations come before the use cases that depend on them.

    What happens after the assessment?

    You own the roadmap and can act on it however you choose. Many clients move straight into an FDE pod for the top-ranked use case, using the business case and baseline metrics from the assessment.

    Is the assessment fixed-price?

    Yes. Scope and price are agreed before the work starts, based on the number of departments and workflows included.
