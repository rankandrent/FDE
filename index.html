    How an FDE engagement
     runs from first call to handover

    01

    1. We map workflows, check data readiness, and rank use cases by value and feasibility with your business owners.

      Deliverables: Readiness scorecard, ranked use-case portfolio, roadmap

    2. A pod of three to five engineers joins your tools, ceremonies, and access controls, paired with the engineers who will own the result.

      Deliverables: Team charter, access plan, success metrics

    3. The pod ships the first use case in your environment, scored against evaluation sets drawn from real work.

      Deliverables: Production-ready system, evaluation report, architecture records

    4. Guardrails, review queues, monitoring, and cost controls go live with a limited user group before wider rollout.

      Deliverables: Release plan, dashboards, runbooks

    5. Your engineers take over in stages while the pod moves to the next use case or steps out entirely.

      Deliverables: Ownership transfer plan, enablement sessions

    Frequently asked questions

    What does forward deployed engineering mean?

    Forward deployed engineering puts engineers inside the client’s environment instead of delivering from a distance. They work in your repositories, with your data, and alongside the people who run the workflow. The approach suits AI work because most of the difficulty sits in integration, edge cases, and adoption rather than in the model itself.

    Why a separate FDE site instead of tkxel.com?

    tkxel.com covers the full range of tkxel services. This site focuses on one delivery model: embedded engineering teams that take AI from strategy to production. Both are run by the same company, teams, and certifications.

    Where do I start if I have no AI use cases defined yet?

    Start with the AI strategy and readiness assessment. In two to four weeks it maps your workflows, ranks use cases by value and feasibility, and checks whether your data can support them. Many clients move straight from that roadmap into an FDE pod for the top-ranked use case.

    Do you work only with Claude, or with other models too?

    We work with the models your organisation has approved, including Claude, GPT, and Gemini, through direct APIs or through Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, and Azure. Model choice is made per workflow based on accuracy, cost, and data residency requirements.

    Is forward deployed engineering right for every company?

    Not always. If your need is a standard capability that an off-the-shelf product already covers well, a licence is usually cheaper and faster. FDE pays back when workflows are specific to your business, span several internal systems, or carry risk that needs custom controls. We will say so on the discovery call if a product fits better.

    Which regions do you cover?

    tkxel has offices in the USA, Saudi Arabia, Portugal, and Pakistan. Pods work on-site, remote, or hybrid, and we align working hours with your core team’s time zone.
